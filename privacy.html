Privacy Policy — Recovery Gang Radio

Effective date: October 30, 2026

Recovery Gang Radio is an independent internet radio station. This policy explains what information the station's own software tools access, and what they do with it.

The connector app

The station uses a small connector app (the “Muse Connector”, part of the Recovery Gang Radio project on Google Cloud) to connect to the station's own YouTube channel through Google's official YouTube Data API. The connection has read-only permission. With it, the app can read:

  • basic information about the station's YouTube channel;
  • the status of the station's live broadcasts (for example, whether the stream is live); and
  • messages posted in the live chat during the station's broadcasts.

The station uses this information only to operate the station: to check that its own stream is running, and to receive song requests that listeners send through the live chat.

What the app cannot do

The app's permission is read-only. It cannot upload, edit, or delete videos, and it cannot change anything on the YouTube channel.

How information is handled

Information read through the API is used only to operate Recovery Gang Radio. It is not sold, and it is not shared with third parties for marketing or any other purpose. Live chat messages are read so the station can take song requests; they are not collected into profiles of individual listeners.

Your choices

Listeners take part in live chat under YouTube's own terms and privacy policy. Anyone who does not wish to be read by the station's request tools can simply enjoy the broadcast without posting in chat.
